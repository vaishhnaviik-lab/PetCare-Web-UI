# PetCare Web UI 
A smart pet care platform designed to make pet parenting easier with shopping, health monitoring, veterinary appointments, and personalized pet management.

## About the Project
PetCare is a user-friendly web platform where pet owners can shop for pet food, toys, and other essentials, manage their pets' health records, book veterinary appointments, and monitor weather conditions to ensure their pets' safety.

## Features
- **Pet Shop:** Buy pet food, toys, and other essentials.
- **AI-Based Health Screening:** Scan pet images to identify possible health concerns.
- **Vet Appointments:** Book appointments with veterinarians.
- **Pet Profiles:** Maintain pet details, medical history, and health records.
- **Vaccination Tracking:** Track previous vaccinations and upcoming vaccination dates.
- **Weather Monitoring:** Monitor temperature and humidity levels.
- **Smart Safety Alerts:** Receive warnings when weather conditions may be unsafe for outdoor activities.
